Play the supplied version of Mus: North/South versus East/West. Your partnership
wins when it first reaches the target. Use only your private cards and public
information. No señas or partner-private information is available.

Each seat has four cards. The 40-card deck has four suits of physical ranks
1,2,3,4,5,6,7,10,11,12. For comparisons, 2 = ace (1) and 3 = king (12).
Normalized ranks ascend 1,4,5,6,7,10,11,12; suits never break ties.
Juego values: ace/2 = 1; 3/10/11/12 = 10; other cards = face value.

Mus needs all four votes. Any no mus starts the lances immediately. After unanimous
mus, each seat exchanges 1-4 cards, then voting starts again. Discard indices refer
to the currently displayed hand; kept cards precede replacements. Discard counts
are cumulative within this hand. Recycled discards can return to play.

The named mano starts each lance. Table priority follows NORTH -> EAST -> SOUTH ->
WEST -> NORTH, beginning at mano. Ties favor the earliest eligible seat in that order.
The best eligible individual hand wins for its team; do not combine partners' cards.
Lances run in order:
- Grande: sort normalized ranks descending and compare the first difference.
- Chica: sort ascending and compare the first difference; lower wins.
- Pares: two pairs/four equal > triple > pair. Compare pair/triple ranks only;
  for two pairs compare the higher pair then the lower. Singletons never break ties.
- Juego: total at least 31; strength 31 > 32 > 40 > 37 > 36 > 35 > 34 > 33.
- Punto replaces juego only when nobody has juego; highest total wins.
Examples using normalized ranks: 12,12,4,1 beats 12,11,11,7 at grande (second
card); 1,4,10,12 beats 1,5,6,7 at chica (second-lowest card). At pares, 7,7,7,1
beats 4,4,4,12 because the triple counts and the singleton does not.
Before pares and juego, everyone truthfully declares eligibility. Only eligible
seats bet on them. If only one team qualifies, it wins without betting. No pares
means skip that lance. Both eligible partners' intrinsic values count for the
winning team: pair 1, triple 2, two pairs/four equal 3; juego 31 scores 3, others 2.
Grande/chica/punto passed by all score 1. There is no special juego real.

The Ruleset and Scoring fields in your input define settlement timing. For v2,
accepted numeric bets and unbet awards wait until all lances finish and all cards
are revealed. Settle grande, chica, pares, juego/punto, stopping as soon as a team
reaches target. Called grande/chica pay the stake; called pares/juego add the
winning team's intrinsic values; called punto adds 1. Scores shown already
include paid awards. Pending awards are not included and their winners may be unknown.

Pass declines to open a bet; you remain eligible to win. Bet/raise amounts are
TOTAL stakes, not increments. Opening minimum is 2; v2 raises add at least 2.
The numeric menu ends at the target, but a target-sized bet is still a numeric bet.
The next eligible opponent answers for its entire team; their partner gets no veto.
Call accepts that stake and ends bidding on this lance. Fold concedes the lance
for your team: the other team receives the previous stake, or 1 for an opening
bet, immediately. In v2 it also receives its pares/juego values (or 1 for punto)
at showdown, if the match has not already ended. There is no extra deje on raises.
Example: bet 2, raise to 6, fold pays 2 now to the raiser's team.

Ordago proposes an immediate showdown for the entire match. In v2 it is distinct
from every numeric bet, and allows only call or fold. Calling reveals all cards,
settles this lance for the match and cancels all pending awards. Folding follows
the usual refusal rule. Legacy v1 uses the immediate-scoring contract shown in
its input instead; do not apply v2 timing or intrinsic additions to v1.
